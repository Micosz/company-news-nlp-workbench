01 / THE BASELINE
X-AMR, as supplied.
OR combines the original EID_N and EID_lt graphs and takes connected components. This setting disables syn_map / VerbNet. Gold labels are scoped by topic for every method.
X-AMR · LREC-COLING 2024 ↗
02 / THE EXTENSIONS
Text helps—and can overmerge.
OR + TF-IDF adds unique mutual-best cluster links with cosine ≥ 0.50, a matching roleset and matching nonempty ARG0 or ARG1. Five-fold train CV chose this preset. The NB guard ranks weak cross-document EID_lt-only links using TF-IDF cosine deciles and balanced CategoricalNB; CV chose no pruning.
03 / THE CLAIM
Exploration, with context.
On dev, OR + TF-IDF CoNLL is 79.0750% / 77.4298% for Ann1 / Ann2. Ann1 CoNLL and both Pair F1 scores decrease versus OR. Dev has been explored, and test_common was previously viewed. These results do not establish consistent superiority, significance, or validated extraction from raw news.
